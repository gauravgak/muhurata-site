What Muhurata is

Muhurata calculates a Vedic birth chart from the details you provide and returns a reading based on classical texts. Planetary positions are computed from standard ephemeris data using sidereal calculation with Lahiri ayanamsa. That part is arithmetic and is the same everywhere.

What a reading is, and is not

Interpretation is drawn from a tradition. It is a reading, not a forecast, and not a statement of fact about your future. Nothing here is medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. Do not make a decision that affects your health, your money or your safety on the basis of a reading alone. If you need professional advice, get it from a professional.

Accuracy of your details

A chart is only as good as the birth time it is built from. If your time of birth is wrong, the ascendant will be wrong and so will much of the reading. We calculate from what you give us and cannot verify it.

Using the service

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Submit only your own details, or details you have permission to submit.
  • Do not scrape, resell or automate access to the service.

Messages

If you opt in, we send your reading and occasional notes about timings relevant to your chart on WhatsApp. Reply STOP at any time and we will stop.

Availability

This is an early service run by a small team. It may be unavailable, and readings may change as the interpretation is refined with input from practising astrologers.
